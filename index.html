Smart Skip — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Smart Skip is designed to be private by default. This policy explains, in plain language, exactly what the extension does and does not do with your data.

The short version

  • Smart Skip does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your data.
  • There are no analytics, no telemetry, no tracking, and no remote servers.
  • Everything the extension stores stays on your device, inside your browser's local extension storage.
  • You can view, export, or permanently delete all of it at any time.

What the extension stores (locally only)

To provide its features, Smart Skip saves the following using the browser's chrome.storage.local API. This data never leaves your computer.

  1. Settings — your feature toggles (auto-skip, mute, speed-up, visual feedback, debug logging, hide watched videos / Mixes, monthly history email and the optional address it goes to) and any per-channel exceptions you create.
  2. Aggregate statistics — counts of how many ads were handled today, over time, and a per-category tally.
  3. Ad history — a local, size-limited log of ads the extension acted on. For each ad this may include: a timestamp and date, the detected advertiser or brand (when shown on screen), the host video's title and URL, the channel name, an estimated ad duration, whether it was skipped or muted, the playback speed used, the ad type (skippable / non-skippable), and a locally inferred category (e.g. "Gaming", "Finance", "Tech").
  4. Watched videos (only if you turn on "Hide watched Mixes") — the YouTube video IDs of videos you open, and of the videos on your own YouTube watch history page, each with a time, so the extension can hide Mixes that start from a song you've already played. No titles, channels or other details are kept, the list is capped to the most recent 5,000 videos, and nothing is recorded while the option is off.
  5. Monthly history email state (only if you turn on "Email my YouTube history monthly") — the month you last exported and, if you chose "Later", when to ask again.

The ad-category inference runs entirely on your device using a built-in keyword model. No text is sent anywhere to be classified.

What the extension does NOT do

  • It does not create an account or profile.
  • It does not set or store cookies, and uses no web beacons or fingerprinting.
  • It does not contact any Smart Skip server, advertising network, or analytics provider. Smart Skip has no servers.
  • It does not read your YouTube password or account details.
  • It reads your YouTube watch history only if you turn on "Hide watched Mixes" or the monthly history email, and only from youtube.com itself, using your existing YouTube session in your browser, the same way YouTube's History page loads it. To read more than the first page, it signs the request with YouTube's standard signature, computed in your browser from your YouTube session cookie; the cookie is never stored and never sent anywhere but youtube.com. The only viewing data kept is the on-device list described above.
  • It never sends email. The monthly history email saves a file to your Downloads folder and opens a Gmail draft in a new tab; you decide whether to attach the file and press Send.
  • It does not inject third-party code. Its only network requests are the optional watch-history reads from youtube.com described above.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • storage — to save your settings, the local ad history/statistics and the optional watched-videos list described above.
  • Host access to *://*.youtube.com/* — so the content script can detect and act on ads while you are watching YouTube. The extension runs only on YouTube pages and nowhere else.

No other permissions are requested.

Your control over your data

  • View — the "Ad Insights" page shows your history, category breakdown, and statistics.
  • Export — the "Export data" button downloads a JSON file of your local data to your computer. This is a local download; nothing is uploaded.
  • Delete — "Clear history" (Ad Insights) or "Reset statistics & history" (Settings) permanently deletes the stored data, and "Forget list" (Settings) deletes the watched-videos list. Turning "Hide watched Mixes" off stops it from being rebuilt. Uninstalling the extension also removes all of it.

Data retention

The ad history is automatically capped (oldest entries are removed once the log grows beyond its limit), daily statistics are retained for a rolling window, and the watched-videos list keeps only the 5,000 most recent videos. Nothing is retained off your device because nothing is ever sent off your device.

Children's privacy

Smart Skip does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will ship with the extension and the "Last updated" date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised through the extension's support channel on its Chrome Web Store listing.
